# Save8 assets

Logos, icons and product images collected from the live save8.ai results page on 12 Oct 2026. Use them for designs and prototypes (Figma file "Result-page", localhost mockups).

## Folders

| Folder | What | Size / format | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `logos/stores/` | 22 store logos | 128×128 PNG | logo.dev, as loaded by save8.ai |
| `logos/banks/` | Card issuer logos: Amazon Pay ICICI, SBI Card, Standard Chartered, Axis, HDFC | 96×96 PNG | assets.save8.ai (HDFC from the local prototype) |
| `logos/gift-cards/` | Amazon shopping, Fresh, Pay, Prime, Prime 12 months tiles | PNG, original size | assets.myhubble.money |
| `logos/save8/` | Save8 wordmark and app icon | PNG (app icon 192×192) | save8.ai |
| `icons/` | Icons used on the results page | SVG, `currentColor` | Copied from save8.ai markup (Heroicons-style) |
| `products/` | Apple iPhone 15 128GB photos in Black, Blue and Pink | 1100×1100 JPG | Black and Blue: Amazon.in product images. Pink: Apple product image, cropped, background whitened |

## Icons

`close`, `menu`, `more-horizontal`, `chevron-down`, `chevron-down-thin`, `chevron-right`, `chevron-right-thin`, `chevron-up`, `location-pin`, `external-link`, `send-arrow-up`, `card-offer-arrow`, `share`. They use `currentColor`, so they take the text colour of wherever they're placed. `menu` is the Heroicons "bars-3" icon; save8.ai draws its menu icon with CSS.

## Notes

- Brand logos belong to their companies. Use them for internal design work only.
- The store logos come from logo.dev through save8.ai's key, which only works on save8.ai. That's why they're saved here rather than linked.
- To add a new store logo, open it on save8.ai and export it from the Figma file, which already has every logo above applied.
